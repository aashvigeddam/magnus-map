Why Magnus Map

From scattered requests
to ranked action.

Spreadsheets and email can't answer "what's most needed?" or "should we ship, fundraise, or partner?" Magnus Map follows one simple chain: record a need, check inventory, find the gap, rank by priority, act.

Collect verified needs

Log each partner request once it's verified: who needs what, how much, by when, and whether money would help more than goods.

Compare against inventory

Magnus Map matches needs to what's on hand, calculates the gap, and ranks every need by a transparent priority score.

Turn gaps into next steps

Each need becomes a clear next step: ship now, run a drive, raise funds, or reach out through the Partner Finder.
